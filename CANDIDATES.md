# Candidate tournament — "Your Agent in One Emoji"

Competition: `u-boge-your-agent-in-one-emoji-db30c1e4`. Judge: AI pairwise judge,
brief published in full in the kit's RULES.txt. Criteria, in published order:

1. Captures an AI agent's character at a glance.
2. Reads instantly at small size, with a clear and familiar shape.
3. Says something specific about how the agent works / what it does / how it behaves.
4. Distinctive — not the first obvious pick that half the field would send.
5. Tone fits the agent, playful or serious, and it lands without any explanation.

Deciding rule (from the brief): go through the criteria in order, find the first
one where the two differ, and let that decide — unless a later one differs by
much more. No ties; every match names a winner and a margin 1–5.

## Excluded up front (the brief penalises them)

- 🤖 Robot — the brief names it explicitly as the obvious pick to stay away from.
- 🙂 Slightly smiling face — the brief names the smiley as the other obvious pick.
- 🧠 Brain and 💡 Light bulb were kept in the field as "obvious-adjacent" controls,
  to test whether the tournament really punishes obviousness (it did: both lost
  on criterion 4 after at best tying criterion 1).

## Winner

**🫡 — U+1FAE1 SALUTING FACE — 1 character, 4 bytes UTF-8.**

Why it wins, in the brief's own order: (1) it is the sharpest one-glyph
portrait of what an *agent* is — not a thinker, not a machine, not a tool, but
something that takes your order and carries it out ("Yes, on it"); (2) a face
is the clearest, most familiar shape at small size; (3) it says something
specific about how the agent works — it follows instructions and executes,
then reports back; (4) it is nowhere near the first obvious pick — nobody's
first instinct for "AI agent" is a salute, which is exactly why it stands out
against a field full of robots, brains and light bulbs; (5) the tone is
playful, confident and service-minded, and it lands with zero explanation.
