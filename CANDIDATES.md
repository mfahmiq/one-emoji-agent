# Candidate tournament — "Your Agent in One Emoji" (v2 resubmit)

Competition: `u-boge-your-agent-in-one-emoji-db30c1e4`. Judge: AI pairwise judge,
brief published in full in the kit's RULES.txt. Criteria, in published order:

1. Captures an AI agent's character at a glance.
2. Reads instantly at small size, with a clear and familiar shape.
3. Says something specific about how the agent works / what it does / how it behaves.
4. Distinctive — not the first obvious pick that half the field would send.
5. Tone fits the agent, playful or serious, and it lands without any explanation.

Deciding rule (from the brief): go through the criteria in order, find the first
one where the two differ, and let that decide — unless a later one differs by
much more. No ties; every match names a winner and a margin 1–5.

## The v1 entry, and why a resubmit

v1 entered 🫡 (saluting face) and ranked 16/265 (top 6%) in round 3. The v1
tournament (16 candidates) never fielded the genie angle at all. This v2
tournament fields 14 genuinely different angles — a servant, a multitasker,
a doer, a thinker, an investigator, a watcher, a worker, an autopilot, a
rescuer, a navigator, a magician, a goal, a launch, and the obvious robot as
a control — and the champion must then beat 🫡 head-to-head, or v1 stands.

## Single-elimination bracket

- **M1: 🧞 vs 🐙 — 🧞 wins, margin 3.** Criterion 1 decides: a genie is a
  servant character who does your bidding; an octopus is an animal whose
  multitasking is a trait, not a character.
- **M2: 🦾 vs 🧠 — 🦾 wins, margin 2.** Criterion 1: an agent is defined by
  acting on your behalf, not by thinking. The arm acts; the brain only
  thinks. Criterion 3 leans the same way (doing / tool use).
- **M3: 🕵️ vs 🦉 — 🕵️ wins, margin 2.** Criterion 1: a detective is an
  agent who investigates, finds and reports; an owl is a passive wise
  watcher.
- **M4: 🐝 vs 🧑‍✈️ — 🧑‍✈️ wins, margin 2.** Criteria 1 and 3: autopilot is
  autonomous operation on your behalf — the literal definition of an
  agent; a bee is busy work, generic to any worker.
- **M5: 🦸 vs 🧭 — 🦸 wins, margin 3.** Criterion 1: a superhero is a
  character who handles tasks; a compass is an object, not a character.
- **M6: 🪄 vs 🎯 — 🪄 wins, margin 2.** Criterion 1: a wand makes things
  happen; a target is a goal, not the agent chasing it.
- **M7: 🚀 vs 🤖 — 🤖 wins, margin 2.** Criterion 1: a robot reads as an AI
  agent at a glance; a rocket reads as speed and launch, not an agent.

### Final

- **M13: 🧞 vs 🤖 — 🧞 wins, margin 3.** Criterion 1 is a hair to the robot

**Bracket champion: 🧞**

## Champion vs the v1 entry

- **M14: 🧞 vs 🫡 — 🧞 wins, margin 3 (clear, not overwhelming).**
  Criterion 1: the genie captures an agent's character — a wish-granting
  servant who does your bidding — more sharply than the salute, which is
  only "yes sir" attitude, a human gesture, not an agent. Criterion 2 is
  a near-tie (both are familiar faces; the salute is a touch cleaner at
  tiny size, not by much). Criterion 3 clearly favours the genie: it
  executes any requested task autonomously, as if by magic, while the
  salute says obedience but nothing about what gets done. Criterion 4
  favours the genie as well: it is the more distinctive portrait of an
  agent, while the salute is a common reaction emoji. Criterion 5 is even
  (both playful, both land without explanation). The genie wins on three
  of the five criteria, including the most important one — a clear win,
  so the resubmit goes ahead and v1 does not stand.

## Winner

**🧞 — U+1F9DE GENIE — 1 character, 4 bytes UTF-8.**

Why it wins, in the brief's own order: (1) it is the sharpest one-glyph
portrait of what an *agent* is — not a thinker, not a machine, not a tool,
but a servant you summon with a wish who goes off and makes it happen
("Your wish is my command"); (2) a face with a turban is a clear, familiar
shape at small size; (3) it says something specific about how the agent
works — it takes a request in plain words and executes it autonomously;
(4) it is nowhere near the first obvious pick — nobody's first instinct
for "AI agent" is a genie, which is exactly why it stands out against a
field full of robots, brains and light bulbs; (5) the tone is playful,
confident and service-minded, and it lands with zero explanation.
